    Where To Stay

    A few places nearby where you can book a room for the night. Rivervale Barn is at Mill Lane, Yateley GU46 7SS, and every option below is a short drive away. Rooms near the venue can get booked up around wedding dates, so we'd recommend reserving yours as early as you can.

    Approx. 4 min drive · in Yateley

    The Casa Hotel

    Handford Lane, Yateley, Hampshire GU46 6BT

    The closest hotel to the barn and a long-time favourite with Rivervale guests, with its own Italian restaurant on site. Very handy for taxis to and from the venue.

    Discounted rate

    Double deluxe at £100 per night including breakfast.

    1 family room available.

    11 am check out.

    Approx. 6 min drive

    The Ely Hotel

    London Road, Blackwater, Yateley, Hampshire GU17 9LJ

    A traditional English country pub with comfortable en-suite rooms on the edge of Yateley, with its own bar, restaurant and free parking. A relaxed, good-value option close by.

    Book / Enquire

    Approx. 8 min drive

    Warbrook House Hotel

    The Street, Eversley, Hampshire RG27 0PL

    A Grade II listed country house set in 121 acres of gardens — a characterful place to stay just a few minutes from the venue.

    Book / Enquire

    Getting home & parking

    Taxis: the bar closes at 11:30 pm, so if you're heading off that night we'd suggest pre-booking a taxi for around then. Rivervale Barn keeps a list of preferred local taxi firms on their contact page. It's always worth booking in advance on a wedding night.

    Driving: you're welcome to leave your car in the venue car park overnight (at your own risk) and collect it by 10:00 am the next morning — the gates open at 8:00 am.
